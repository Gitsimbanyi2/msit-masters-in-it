# MSCIT91312 — Research Methodology for Information Systems

## Purpose

Design a defensible IS research project: question, method, data, ethics, and write-up — the path into the dissertation.

## Topic map

- What is information systems research vs a technical project report
- Positivist, interpretive, design-science approaches
- Problem statement, objectives, hypotheses / research questions
- Literature review and referencing (APA or the school standard)
- Sampling, surveys, interviews, experiments, artefact evaluation
- Validity, reliability, bias
- Ethics and data protection for human subjects
- Proposal structure that UoK examiners expect

## Folder

- `notes.md` — your weekly notes
- `assignments/` — CAT scans, practicals, marked work (PDFs gitignored by default)
- `revision.md` — last-week checklist

## Exam / CAT tips

- A method without a question is busywork
- Design-science: build an artefact AND evaluate it
- Keep a research log from week one of the dissertation module
