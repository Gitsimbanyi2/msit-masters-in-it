# MSCIT91311 — E-Governance and Service Delivery

## Purpose

Design digital public services that are usable, lawful, and secure for citizens and institutions.

## Topic map

- E-government stages: information, interaction, transaction, transformation
- Irembo-style one-stop service thinking (concept, not a product dump)
- Identity, civil registry, interoperability, APIs between agencies
- Inclusion: language, disability, rural connectivity
- Data protection, records, audit
- Cybersecurity of public portals (availability + integrity of records)
- Change management and digital literacy
