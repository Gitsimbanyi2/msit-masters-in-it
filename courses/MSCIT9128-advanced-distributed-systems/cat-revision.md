## Section A pattern (compulsory case, ~50 marks)

Typical firm: many branches + warehouse + head office, mixed OS, no middleware yet, a six-hour outage, inconsistent price/loyalty caches.

Expect:

1. Definition, hardware vs software, advantages, challenges
2. Transparency, openness, scalability, and why replicas drifted
3. NOS / DOS / middleware and client–server vs P2P
4. Multiprocessor, SMP/ASMP, shared memory vs messages, heterogeneity
5. A diagram: layers, or CS vs P2P, or 3-tier

## Section B pattern (choose questions)

Cloud models, bursting, SOA vs 3-tier, proxy, POSDCORB, hierarchy. Always map the theory back to the case (tills, warehouse, HO, online shop).
